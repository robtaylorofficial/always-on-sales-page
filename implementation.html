Month 1
Setup & Configuration
👋
Stage 1 Day 1 to Week 1

Sign Up

You complete the Always On onboarding form and your account is created. A brief setup call confirms your commercial priorities, team structure and integration requirements, then configuration begins.

Onboarding form completed
Account and tenant created
Business priorities agreed
Integration requirements confirmed
Team contacts and access levels set
⚙️
Stage 2 Week 1–2

Business Onboarding

Your Always On environment is configured around your business, not a generic template. Integrations are connected, your brand identity is applied, and routing logic is built to reflect how your team actually works.

CRM integration: connect your existing CRM so leads flow in automatically with full context
Email sync: Gmail and Outlook connected per rep via secure OAuth; no passwords stored
WhatsApp Business: inbound WhatsApp enquiries routed and handled by Sally
Microsoft Teams: internal alerts and notifications sent directly to your team channel
Web form embedding: Always On capture forms live on your website within days
Calendar connections: rep calendars linked for meeting booking and follow-up prompts
Branding: your logo, colours and tone of voice applied across all agent communications
Personalisation: agent names, sign-offs and language tuned to sound like your business
Rep profiles: individual rep details, territories, and specialisms loaded for accurate routing
Routing rules: lead assignment logic built around sector, geography, product line or capacity
Escalation paths: high-value or urgent enquiries flagged immediately to the right person
Data & compliance: DPA signed, data flows confirmed, GDPR controls in place
🧠
Stage 3 Week 2–3

Knowledge Sharing

Sally, Dan and Caroline need to know your business before they can represent it well. This stage is about building the knowledge base that makes your agents sound like they have been in your business for years, not a generic AI on a landing page.

Product and service catalogue uploaded
Pricing frameworks and key commercial terms
FAQs, common objections and preferred responses
Competitor context and positioning
Territory structures and rep specialisms
Key customer types and qualification criteria
Brand voice, tone guidelines and language to avoid
Escalation triggers and VIP account rules
🔬
Stage 4 Week 3–4

Testing Period

Before anything goes live, everything is stress-tested. Your onboarding manager runs structured test scenarios across every integration and every agent. You review outputs, flag anything that feels off, and we refine until it is right.

Form submissions tested across all entry points
Routing logic validated against real enquiry types
Sally response quality reviewed and refined
Dan's meeting prep and pipeline briefings tested
Caroline's digest outputs reviewed by leadership
CRM data flow verified end to end
Email and WhatsApp response speed benchmarked
Edge cases and unusual enquiry types tested

Stage 5 Week 5, early Month 2

Team Onboarding

Your sales team is introduced to Always On in a short, practical session, not a feature walkthrough, but a focused briefing on what changes for them day to day. This keeps adoption high and resistance low.

Live team session covering the dashboard and agents
Sally's inbound handling explained to the team
Dan's daily prompts and how to use them
Caroline's digest cadence set up for leadership
CRM handoff process demonstrated
Rep-level access and notification preferences set
Escalation and routing review with managers
Questions answered; knowledge base refined based on team feedback
